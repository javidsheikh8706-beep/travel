Frequently Asked Questions

Can outside Srinagar cabs do local sightseeing inside Sonamarg? +

Srinagar-registered commercial cabs can drop you at your Sonamarg hotel, but local sightseeing points (Thajiwas Glacier base, Baltal, Zero Point, and Fish Point) require local Sonamarg union-registered cabs. Booking directly with us covers both highway transfers and local sightseeing seamlessly.

How long does the Srinagar Airport to Sonamarg drive take? +

The distance is approximately 85–90 km via NH-1 (Ganderbal – Kangan – Gagangir) and takes 2.5 to 3 hours depending on traffic and weather conditions.

Do you provide 4x4 SUVs with snow chains in winter? +

Yes. Between November and April, we deploy 4x4 Scorpio/Innova vehicles fitted with anti-skid snow chains whenever Gagangir or Sonamarg experiences fresh snowfall.
